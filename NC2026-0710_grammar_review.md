# NC2026-0710 全文语法检查建议

检查对象：`NC2026-0710.pdf`  
检查范围：全文 15 页  
说明：以下结果基于 PDF 抽取文本逐句检查；已忽略 PDF 抽取导致的断词、公式粘连、标题缺空格、参考文献排版粘连等伪问题，只列出真实语法错误或明显学术表达问题。

---

## Page 2

### 1. Verb form after “to”

**原文**  
“this is the first study to fairly compares representative BMCI algorithms”

**问题**  
`to` 后应接动词原形，`compares` 应改为 `compare`。

**建议修改**  
“this is the first study to fairly **compare** representative BMCI algorithms”

---

### 2. Comparative construction

**原文**  
“achieves superior dereverberation performance to weighted prediction error...”

**问题**  
`superior performance to` 搭配不自然；更常用 `compared with`，或直接用 `outperforms`。

**建议修改**  
“achieves superior dereverberation performance **compared with** weighted prediction error...”

或：

“**outperforms** weighted prediction error...”

---

## Page 3

### 3. Awkward participial phrase

**原文**  
“having a faster convergence rate and tracking speed than the traditional NMCFLMS algorithm”

**问题**  
分词结构略悬垂；`tracking speed` 与 `convergence rate` 并列也不太自然。

**建议修改**  
“**which has a faster convergence rate and faster tracking than** the traditional NMCFLMS algorithm”

---

## Page 4

### 4. Non-standard word “Insteadly”

**原文**  
“Insteadly, audio signals are colored, non-stationary, and time-varying.”

**问题**  
`Insteadly` 不是标准英语。

**建议修改**  
“**In contrast**, audio signals are colored, non-stationary, and time-varying.”

---

### 5. Missing article before technical term

**原文**  
“which is obtained by computing time difference of arrival (TDOA)”

**问题**  
技术术语前缺少冠词。

**建议修改**  
“which is obtained by computing **the time difference of arrival** (TDOA)”

---

### 6. Awkward “method combining” phrase

**原文**  
“a robust MINT-based dereverberation method combining BMCI algorithms”

**问题**  
`method combining` 略生硬；而且语义上是结合 MINT 和 BMCI 算法。

**建议修改**  
“a robust MINT-based dereverberation method **that combines MINT with BMCI algorithms**”

---

## Page 5

### 7. Article use with “previous research”

**原文**  
“Following the previous research”

**问题**  
`previous research` 在这里通常作不可数名词，不需要 `the`。

**建议修改**  
“**Following previous research**”

---

### 8. Preposition for noisy environments

**原文**  
“For noisy environments, the additive noise signal v(n) is incorporated...”

**问题**  
`For noisy environments` 在此处不够地道。

**建议修改**  
“**In noisy environments**, the additive noise signal v(n) is incorporated...”

---

## Page 6

### 9. Missing article/plural marking

**原文**  
“In high-noise case”

**问题**  
缺少冠词或复数形式。

**建议修改**  
“**In high-noise cases**”

或：

“**Under high-noise conditions**”

---

### 10. Missing article before algorithm name

**原文**  
“the update equation of RNMCFLMS algorithm is”

**问题**  
算法名称前缺少冠词。

**建议修改**  
“the update equation of **the RNMCFLMS algorithm** is”

---

## Page 7

### 11. Missing article before algorithm name

**原文**  
“To address this issue, ℓp-RNMCFLMS algorithm [11] replaces...”

**问题**  
算法名称前缺少冠词。

**建议修改**  
“To address this issue, **the ℓp-RNMCFLMS algorithm** [11] replaces...”

---

### 12. Unidiomatic noun phrase

**原文**  
“their sparsity degree varies across different acoustic environments”

**问题**  
`sparsity degree` 不够地道。

**建议修改**  
“their **degree of sparsity** varies across different acoustic environments”

---

### 13. Non-standard academic wording

**原文**  
“the squared summation of the second-order numerical differentiation”

**问题**  
`squared summation` 不是常见学术表达，语义不够清晰。

**建议修改**  
“the **sum of squares of the second-order numerical derivative**”

---

## Page 9

### 14. Article/preposition issue

**原文**  
“Four NMCFLMS-based BMCI algorithms described in the last section are used for iterative baselines”

**问题**  
缺少定冠词；`used for iterative baselines` 搭配不对；`last section` 建议改为 `previous section`。

**建议修改**  
“**The four NMCFLMS-based BMCI algorithms described in the previous section are used as iterative baselines**”

---

## Page 10

### 15. Missing article

**原文**  
“For dereverberation task”

**问题**  
缺少冠词。

**建议修改**  
“**For the dereverberation task**”

---

### 16. Missing preposition

**原文**  
“The second RIR was truncated 1024 taps”

**问题**  
缺少介词 `to`。

**建议修改**  
“The second RIR was truncated **to** 1024 taps”

---

### 17. Awkward modifier placement

**原文**  
“in the same evaluation following [12]”

**问题**  
修饰关系不清，表达不自然。

**建议修改**  
“**in the same evaluation setting as in [12]**”

或：

“**using the same evaluation setting following [12]**”

---

### 18. Sentence fragment caused by “While”

**原文**  
“While ℓp-RNMCFLMS and Eℓp-RNMCFLMS algorithms adopt a set of p values...”

**问题**  
`While` 引导从句后缺少主句，形成句子片段。

**建议修改**  
“**The ℓp-RNMCFLMS and Eℓp-RNMCFLMS algorithms adopt a set of p values...**”

---

### 19. Sentence fragment caused by “While”

**原文**  
“While swept-sine measurement and blind estimation suffer from different source distortions and inherent scale ambiguity, making accurate waveform comparison between the measurements and estimates difficult.”

**问题**  
`While` 导致句子片段；后半部分不是完整主句。

**建议修改**  
“**Swept-sine measurement and blind estimation suffer from different source distortions and inherent scale ambiguity, making accurate waveform comparison between the measurements and estimates difficult.**”

---

## Page 11

### 20. Awkward noun phrase

**原文**  
“under varying estimate lengths”

**问题**  
`estimate lengths` 作为名词短语不自然。

**建议修改**  
“under varying **estimation lengths**”

或更准确：

“under varying **estimated filter lengths**”

---

### 21. Unidiomatic use of “speech”

**原文**  
“using the simulated 256-tap RIR with a WGN sequence, a TIMIT English speech and a THCHS-30 Mandarin speech.”

**问题**  
`a TIMIT English speech` / `a THCHS-30 Mandarin speech` 不地道；这里应使用 `utterance`。

**建议修改**  
“using the simulated 256-tap RIR with a WGN sequence, **a TIMIT English utterance, and a THCHS-30 Mandarin utterance**.”

---

## Page 12

### 22. Wrong phrase: “prefer to”

**原文**  
“Here, MINT-N/R/ℓp/Eℓ prefer to MINT using RIRs estimated by NMCFLMS-class algorithms.”

**问题**  
`prefer to` 用错，应为 `refer to`。

**建议修改**  
“Here, MINT-N/R/ℓp/Eℓ **refer to** MINT using RIRs estimated by NMCFLMS-class algorithms.”

---

## Page 13

### 23. Pronoun agreement

**原文**  
“PESQ scores of MINT-N/MINT-R are misleading in reflecting its dereverberation performance”

**问题**  
主语是复数方法 `MINT-N/MINT-R`，代词不应使用单数 `its`。

**建议修改**  
“PESQ scores of MINT-N/MINT-R are misleading in reflecting **their** dereverberation performance.”

---

### 24. Repeated “in” phrases

**原文**  
“outperforms WPE and GWPE in the dereverberation task in noise-free and low-SNR cases in PESQ.”

**问题**  
连续多个 `in` 短语，句子笨重且不自然。

**建议修改**  
“outperforms WPE and GWPE **in terms of PESQ on the dereverberation task under noise-free and low-SNR conditions**.”

---

## 未列为语法错误的内容

以下内容未作为语法错误列入：

- PDF 抽取导致的单词粘连，例如 `signal.Overthepastfewdecades`
- 公式附近缺空格，例如 `L= 1024samples`
- 图表、算法框、参考文献中因排版抽取导致的连写
- 技术术语本身，如 `with respect to the first channel`、`sampled at 16 kHz` 等，这些表达本身可接受

---

## 总结

全文共发现 **24 处** 真实语法或明显学术表达问题，建议优先修改以下高影响问题：

1. `compares` → `compare`
2. `Insteadly` → `In contrast`
3. `prefer to` → `refer to`
4. 两处 `While ...` 句子片段
5. 多处算法名称前缺少 `the`
6. `truncated 1024 taps` → `truncated to 1024 taps`
