Subjective listening evaluation

Subjective Scoring for Dereverberation Examples

All dereverberated examples begin at a neutral midpoint so that listeners can assess the true perceived quality. After scoring, please complete the listener information at the bottom, and the results will be automatically sent to the organizers.

Scoring rubric

5 ExcellentClean, natural, little audible reverberation or noise.
4 GoodClearly improved; mild residual reverberation/noise or slight coloration.
3 FairUseful dereverberation, but artifacts, residual noise, or coloration remain noticeable.
2 PoorLimited improvement; reverberation/noise remains strong or speech is distorted.
1 BadSevere reverberation/noise/distortion; difficult to judge speech quality.

Recommended listening order: WPE/GWPE → BMCI-MINT methods → MINT-Reference. Use headphones and compare examples within the same SNR.
